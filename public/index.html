FAQ

Common questions answered

What is NanoID?

NanoID is a modern, secure, URL-friendly unique ID generator. It produces IDs with similar uniqueness guarantees as UUID v4 but is faster, more compact (21 vs 36 characters), and URL-safe.

Can I generate multiple IDs at once?

Yes! You can generate up to 10 IDs at a time. Perfect for seeding databases or creating test data.

What's the difference between UUID v4 and v7?

UUID v4 is completely random. UUID v7 includes a timestamp prefix, making IDs sortable by time while maintaining uniqueness. Use v7 when you need chronological ordering.

Is ULID better than NanoID?

ULID is time-sortable like UUID v7 but URL-safe like NanoID. It's 26 characters vs NanoID's 21. Use ULID if you need time-ordering, NanoID for maximum compactness.
